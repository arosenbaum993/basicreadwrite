Weeks 1-3 | P.EICC

Unit 1: Building the Literacy Lab

Launch routines, learn student reading profiles, and help students set personal literacy goals.

Reading

Diagnostics, interest survey, and short high-interest texts.

Writing

Reading identity narrative and baseline sample.

Language

Vocabulary screener and decoding checks.

Talk

Norms, listening stems, and partner share.

Assessment

Diagnostic snapshot and baseline rubric.

Mini-Lesson Moves

  • Model rereading when meaning breaks down.
  • Build a reading identity timeline.
  • Teach accountable talk stems.

Teacher-Created Materials

  • Reading survey and goal card.
  • Diagnostic conference form.
  • Three short choice texts.

Student Tasks

  • Complete survey and goal card.
  • Write a reading identity narrative.
  • Share one strength and one support needed.

Checks

  • Baseline writing rubric.
  • Conference notes.
  • Exit ticket on reading habits.

Supports

  • Sentence frames, audio text, and one strength-one need grouping notes.

Weeks 4-6 | Foundations + Narrative Techniques

Unit 2: Decoding & Comprehension Basics

Strengthen word attack, fluency, retelling, and summary habits.

Reading

Main idea, summary, context clues, repeated reading.

Writing

Summary paragraph and short narrative.

Language

Prefixes, suffixes, roots, and Tier 2 words.

Talk

Partner retell and turn-and-talk.

Assessment

Comprehension checks and fluency probe.

Mini-Lesson Moves

  • Chunk multisyllabic words.
  • Use somebody-wanted-but-so-then summaries.
  • Think aloud context clues.

Materials

  • Narrative passages.
  • Word study list.
  • Fluency reflection chart.

Tasks

  • Annotate character, problem, and resolution.
  • Write a five-sentence summary.
  • Read one passage three times and graph growth.

Checks

  • Weekly comprehension quiz.
  • Fluency accuracy, rate, and expression notes.

Supports

  • Chunked passages, color-coded affixes, and oral rehearsal.

Weeks 7-9 | L.GC + P.ST

Unit 3: Reading-Writing Connection

Turn short reading into focused response writing with correct sentences.

Reading

Informational texts linked to core English.

Writing

Claim, evidence, explanation paragraph.

Language

Fragments, run-ons, agreement.

Talk

Small-group discussion with evidence.

Assessment

Q1 benchmark paragraph.

Mini-Lessons

  • Underline prompt, mark evidence, draft claim.
  • Repair fragments and run-ons.
  • Introduce evidence with simple phrases.

Materials

  • Two short informational texts.
  • Paragraph exemplar.
  • Sentence repair set.

Tasks

  • Annotate for prompt evidence.
  • Draft and revise a response paragraph.
  • Repair three sentences.

Checks

  • Q1 paragraph rubric.
  • Portfolio reflection.

Supports

  • Paragraph frames, sentence strips, and evidence-explanation organizer.

Reading

Structure-based expository paragraphs.

  • Use headings and transitions as clues.

Materials

  • Signal-word bookmark.
  • Organizer set.

Tasks

  • Sort passages by structure.
  • Complete an organizer.
  • Add precise transitions.

Checks

  • Short essay rubric.

Supports

  • Color-coded transitions and partially completed organizers.

Weeks 13-15 | T.RA

Unit 5: Reading for Evidence

Choose strong evidence and explain how it proves a claim.

Reading

Close reading, annotation, evidence ranking.

Writing

Claim-evidence-explanation paragraph.

Language

Vocabulary in context.

Talk

Evidence hunt and findings presentation.

Assessment

Cited evidence paragraph.

Mini-Lessons

  • Separate interesting details from useful evidence.
  • Introduce, cite, and explain evidence.
  • Annotate claim, evidence, confusion, vocabulary.

Materials

  • Shared article with paragraph numbers.
  • Evidence strength ladder.
  • CEE template.

Tasks

  • Rank four evidence choices.
  • Write a paragraph with two pieces of evidence.
  • Defend one evidence choice.

Checks

  • Evidence paragraph rubric.
  • Annotation check.

Supports

  • Paragraph-number citations, explanation starters, bilingual vocabulary support.

Weeks 22-24 | T.T Argument

Unit 8: Building an Argument

Make a clear claim, support it, and respond to another viewpoint.

Reading

Model arguments and counterclaims.

Writing

Argument paragraph to short essay.

Language

Assert, refute, concede, qualify, justify.

Talk

Structured debate.

Assessment

Argument draft and peer review.

Mini-Lessons

  • Identify claim, reason, evidence, counterclaim, rebuttal.
  • Turn opinion into arguable claim.
  • Practice respectful rebuttal language.

Materials

  • Paired arguments.
  • Vocabulary cards.
  • Claim-counterclaim-rebuttal planner.

Tasks

  • Sort opinion, claim, reason, evidence.
  • Draft rebuttal paragraph.
  • Defend a claim with evidence notes.

Checks

  • Argument rubric.
  • Peer review checklist.

Supports

  • Accessible topics, debate roles, and claim frames.

Weeks 34-36 | Portfolio

Unit 12: Portfolio & Growth Reflection

See growth, revise intentionally, and present reading and writing evidence.

Reading

Reread portfolio evidence.

Writing

Reflective essay and revision.

Language

Cumulative vocabulary and editing goals.

Talk

Portfolio presentation.

Assessment

End diagnostic and final portfolio.

Mini-Lessons

  • Select artifacts showing growth.
  • Reflect using at first, now, because, next.
  • Revise one older paragraph with a current checklist.

Materials

  • Portfolio pieces.
  • Growth organizer.
  • Presentation card.

Tasks

  • Annotate three artifacts.
  • Revise one earlier piece.
  • Present one reading and one writing growth point.

Checks

  • End-of-year diagnostic.
  • Reflection rubric.
  • Portfolio checklist.

Supports

  • Before-and-after samples, reflection frames, and small-group presentation option.
